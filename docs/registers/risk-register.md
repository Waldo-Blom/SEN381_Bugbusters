## Initial Risk Register
In accordance with the CivicConnect Master Project Brief, the Risk Register is maintained as a live engineering artefact and records project-specific risks together with their cause, probability, impact, priority, mitigation, contingency, owner and current status. Risks are identified from the current CivicConnect scope, requirements, constraints and engineering decisions and will be reviewed and updated as new evidence becomes available throughout subsequent milestones. Where a risk occurs, it will be treated as an issue and linked to the relevant engineering actions or decisions.
